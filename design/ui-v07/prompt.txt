Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: a SINGLE proposed desktop game screen for 新药物语, a pharmaceutical research management game. Design study only, not an implementation screenshot.
Primary request: restrained slightly realistic 2.5D office and laboratory, feasible for a small independent game. Avoid the prior toy / clay / miniature dollhouse style. A real modest research workplace with realistic adult proportions, gently simplified art.
Composition: landscape 16:10 game screenshot mockup, fixed elevated orthographic three-quarter camera. A continuous rectangular office floor interior takes about two thirds of the screen, a narrow readable management sidebar takes the remaining third. Two adjacent small rooms connected by an open doorway: a research office with two workstations and a filing shelf; a chemistry/formulation laboratory with a single central workbench, modest glassware, one fume hood and a small instrument. Cutaway front walls, back walls and floor remain. No floating island, no desktop diorama base, no surrounding town. Clean navigable open floor and few modular rectangular props.
People: exactly TWO original adult scientists, normal adult body proportions (roughly seven-heads-tall), one office researcher in smart casual clothes, one lab scientist in lab coat and safety glasses at the bench. Modestly detailed natural faces, not celebrity likenesses. Each silhouette should remain readable and easy to animate. No crowd and no portraits filling the screen.
Style: semi-realistic illustrated game environment, subtle painted textures on white laminate, light grey walls, muted blue-grey cabinets, brushed metal and natural wood desks. Soft daylight, mild ambient shadow, no cinematic fog, no tiny clutter, no round clay furniture, no chibi heads, no fantasy architecture. Objects should have convincing ordinary scale. Practical rather than luxury biotech headquarters.
UI: quiet flat ivory/slate panels, restrained teal active outline around the lab workbench, a small pointer cue to suggest it is selectable. Top bar exact text "新药物语" and "第 1 年 · 第 12 周" and "暂停". Sidebar exact title "处方与工艺", then "小药丸 · 制剂与工艺", then "第 1 / 3 批开发完成", a simple horizontal progress bar, then "工艺稳定 +2.1". Two clearly separated sidebar buttons exact labels "查看团队" and "研发手记". Bottom navigation exact labels "研究所", "团队", "项目", "成果". Small unobtrusive footer exact text "视觉原型 · 尚未接入游戏". Keep other text absent, do not invent tiny illegible information cards. No charts over the characters, no gamified glowing health bars.
Purpose: make the player feel they are managing people working in an actual office and lab, while keeping development scope small. Use screen space efficiently; believable accessible room layout. No logos or watermarks.
